Productive landscape

Production across three time horizons.

0–2 YEARS

Immediate cash flow

  • Millets, pulses and seasonal vegetables
  • Turmeric, ginger and moringa
  • Lemongrass, aloe vera and mushrooms
  • Indigenous dairy and country eggs
3–7 YEARS

Asset creation

  • Mango, sweet lime, guava and amla
  • Honey and apiculture
  • Cold-pressed oils and artisanal ghee
  • Expanding food-forest harvests
8–20+ YEARS

Legacy assets

  • Compliant sandalwood and native timber
  • Mature orchards and bamboo groves
  • A direct-to-consumer farm brand
  • Established woodland habitat

Timelines are indicative, not harvest guarantees. Crop choices follow local agronomy, water budgets and legal compliance.
